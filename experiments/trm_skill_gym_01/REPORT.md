Net: keep visits low, verifier_visits at/near 0, keep latent_steps modest (+1), avoid feedback/ldt/hidden/weight_decay perturbations and avoid verifier-visit escalation.

Visit/verifier/cycle-heavy knobs and latent-step/ldt/feedback-rlt variants mostly hurt or lost the race: repair visits=2 0.4707, curation visits=2 0.6602, research latent_steps=3 0.4121, curation ldt_projection 0.2383, deduction latent_steps=1 0.8789, stopping verifier_visits=2 0.8164, curation feedback=rlt 0.6367.
