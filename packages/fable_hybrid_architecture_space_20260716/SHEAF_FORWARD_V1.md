# Controller-Mesh Sheaf Forward v1

This sidecar records the first staged forward test of the controller-mesh spectral theory. It does not modify the
frozen Fable ZIP.

Calibration-only spectra were constructed for 64 evidence/threshold/fallback genomes. Forty-eight discovery
outcomes fitted a ridge predictor. Predictions for 16 hash-held-out combinations were sealed before their outcomes
were computed.

The registered gate passes: held-out Spearman correlation is `+0.705`, the matched-transport N0 p-value is
`0.0078`, and the predicted top four improve objective by `+0.1476` over the held-out mean. The best actual genome
uses exact evidence with correction infusion.

The important caveat comes from post-hoc controls. An additive categorical genome baseline already reaches
`rho=+0.673`; the spectral increment is small on 16 cases. Intervention-only geometry is weak, stability-only is
moderate, and their combination is strongest. Phase-boundary proximity fails as a utility acquisition rule.

The defensible conclusion is forward predictive value within this panel, not unique spectral information or
causal control. Independent replication with a preregistered categorical comparator is required.

Full math and receipts are in `docs/controller_mesh_sheaf_forward.md` and
`reports/checkpoint_controller_mesh_sheaf_forward_v1.md`. Independent external audit is pending.
