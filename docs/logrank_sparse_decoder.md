# Sparse recurrent factor-graph decoder design

Source: https://github.com/MoralityLabAI/LoopedTransformers/tree/6f4d83449f3378997a41db9c4f05b74aaca9bf23/experiments/logrank_bpd_11.

LOGRANK-BPD-11 uses a known quotient basis, not a learned BPD basis. Synchronous damped sum-product on parity constraints combines repeated observation LLRs, aggregates variable-to-factor evidence, and updates each parity factor by the product of incoming tanh-half-LLRs excluding the destination. At most 60 iterations; product of approximate bit marginals used for query predictions. A separate edge-list implementation reproduced 16,560 marginals within 1.2e-14. On 24 small worlds at 35% noise, 32r observations, sparse BP 709/768 versus dense BP 538/768; exact posterior 740/768 versus 750/768. Sparse BP scales to rank 48 with three of four complete states recovered under this budget.

Proposed adapter: explicit sparse incidence graph, per-edge LLR memory, deterministic bounded loop, exact GF(2) rank tracking, confidence-gated query readout and fallback to small-component exact marginalization. Preserve independent prior/likelihood assumptions and count edge-message FLOPs, storage, local depth, verifier calls and actual inter-module message bits separately. Do not equate an internal floating LLR with one transmitted bit. Before learning a BPD component graph, reproduce supplied-basis baseline; test basis changes that alter factor degree and compare fixed sparse, dense, random and learned graphs at matched observation budgets. No native model integration or causal intervention claimed.
