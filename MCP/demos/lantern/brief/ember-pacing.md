# Saltglass patrol · encounter contract

The Saltglass patrol is tuned for six vigor. Three extinguished sentries must be recovered as embers to open the eastern tide gate. Four foes begin on the map: one guard, one skulk, and two wisps. There is exactly one guard because overlapping guard wind-ups on the narrow route can make an opening impossible.

The alternate encounter uses the same collision, facing, dash, drop, and gate mechanics as the Saltfen. It is a separate roster. Coordinates are integers in world pixels, with x increasing toward the east gate.
