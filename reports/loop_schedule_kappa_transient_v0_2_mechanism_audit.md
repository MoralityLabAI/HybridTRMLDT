# Kappa Transient v0.2 Mechanism Audit

## Finding

The sealed norms resolve the `R=128`, `E=2048` trough as **net destructive interference in the suffix-sensitivity channel**, not loss of gradient coherence. They also reject the literal shared-training-item explanation for the exposure-pinned result. No new model execution was used.

## Probe Decomposition

The registered estimator is

```text
kappa = ||sum_r U_r|| ||sum_r G_r||
        / (R max_r ||U_r|| max_r ||G_r||).
```

For either vector family `v_r`, define

```text
I_v = ||sum_r v_r||^2 / sum_r ||v_r||^2.
```

Then

```text
I_v - 1 = 2 sum_{r<s} <v_r,v_s> / sum_r ||v_r||^2.
```

Thus `I_v<1` is direct evidence that the aggregate pairwise cross-term is negative. It does not establish that every pair is anti-aligned, so this audit uses *net destructive interference* rather than the stronger unqualified term *anti-alignment*.

| Exposure | Step | Geometric kappa | Geometric `I_U` | Geometric `I_G` | Seeds with `I_U<1` |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1024 | 1 | 0.497605 | 0.539124 | 45.311692 | 2/3 |
| 2048 | 2 | 0.182968 | 0.076982 | 42.463174 | 3/3 |
| 4096 | 4 | 0.638521 | 0.752071 | 42.870358 | 3/3 |
| 8192 | 8 | 0.694384 | 0.682480 | 42.617060 | 2/3 |

The gradient channel remains strongly constructive and nearly stationary. The trough is instead a roughly tenfold drop in geometric sensitivity interference from `0.539` to `0.077`, followed by recovery to `0.752`. Seed 101 is the sharpest instance (`I_U=0.00376`), but all three seeds cross the exact negative-cross-term threshold at the registered trough.

## Data-Stream Audit

Training calls the deterministic task generator with `stream=step-1`; exposure is not its stream key.

| R | Step at E=2048 | Stream at E=2048 |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 16 | 16 | 15 |
| 32 | 8 | 7 |
| 64 | 4 | 3 |
| 128 | 2 | 1 |

The `R=64` and `R=128` trough cells therefore consume different batches. A single hard segment at a shared stream position cannot explain their common exposure.

## Residual Confound

The runner's single `seed` controls model initialization, task permutation/signs, training-batch order, measurement construction, and probe direction. Running three new ordinary seeds would therefore conflate all five channels. The discriminating follow-up must introduce a first-class `data_order_seed` while holding the other four fixed.
