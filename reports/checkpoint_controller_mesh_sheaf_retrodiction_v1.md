## Result

128 N0 draws (`p=0.0078`). The signs show that productive gate/fallback disagreement appears as a low mode, so a
larger undifferentiated gap is not automatically a better controller.

The strict kernel-migration prediction fails. Seed 17 has a small positive capture loss (`+0.0034`), while seeds
29 and 43 increase exposed-direction capture (`-0.0030`, `-0.0269`). None beat matched random directions. Post-hoc
signed margins instead show translation across the gate in all seeds, with mean shifts `+0.896`, `+2.223`, and
`+0.310`.
